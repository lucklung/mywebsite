Become a YouthGood.ai Ambassador

YouthGood.ai is a youth-led initiative empowering students worldwide to think critically about artificial intelligence, its ethics, and its impact on society. We are building a global network of student leaders passionate about ethical AI, technology, and positive impact — and we're looking for motivated student leaders like you.
